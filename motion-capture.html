01 / Overview

A camera inside
a virtual world.

This QUT course project brought a nine-person team together across concept development, motion capture, virtual production and post-production.

The production combined performance captured through Vicon Shogun with a scene in Unreal Engine 5.6. I worked on virtual camera operation and the visual treatment of the environment, then edited the final piece in DaVinci Resolve using rendered shots and behind-the-scenes footage filmed by teammates.

My contribution covered on-set virtual cinematography and the final edit of the project film.

01 / Camera operation

Moving through
the scene.

I operated a virtual camera connected to the Vicon and Unreal Engine setup, using a tablet to frame and capture the action in the digital environment. This let me respond to the performers' movement while deciding how the audience would see the scene.

02 / Director of photography

Shaping
the look.

As director of photography, I adjusted lighting and the placement of objects in Unreal Engine to support the composition of the virtual scene. Camera movement, lighting and set elements had to work together around the captured performances.

03 / Post-production

One finished
piece.

In DaVinci Resolve, I edited the rendered Unreal Engine footage together with behind-the-scenes footage captured by other members of the team. The edit connects the virtual film with a view of how the motion capture production was made.
